# Interview points

Short, honest talking points for Jagadish-insurance-claims-agents. Every number quoted is from the
real run shown in the README.

- Straight-through processing only where it is safe: low risk and under $25,000 with matching documents.
- In the real run, a duplicate and inflated claim on a two-day-old policy scored 80 from three cited signals and went to investigation, then a human denied it.
- Every signal cites evidence, because adjusters will not act on a number they cannot explain.
- The ledger makes the whole claim replayable for audit.

## Likely follow-up questions

- How would you set the $25,000 threshold? (From loss-ratio and audit data per line of business.)
- What changes with an LLM extractor? (Keep the confidence and snippet contract, and add a low-confidence review route.)
- How do you avoid unfair outcomes? (Monitor tier rates by segment and keep humans on every denial.)
